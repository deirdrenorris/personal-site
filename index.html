About

I love building businesses and products that solve big problems elegantly, and lean, high-performing teams that turn product-market fit into durable, compounding growth.

My story isn't a straight line. I was an editor of the humor magazine and a varsity athlete in college, started my career in finance and strategy, then found tech product management to be the place where my skills could shine and have the most impact. I am rigorous about data, relentless about execution, deeply curious and empathetic, and genuinely invested in the organizations I work with.

I've built and led across growth and hypergrowth-stage companies (SurveyMonkey, Bird) and earlier-stage startups, from pre-product to IPO, leading everything from Growth to 0-to-1 launches to transformations that set companies up for their next chapter. I've been working with ML and AI for more than a decade and have seen everything from bad to mediocre and great.

I'm looking for the next great team to join as a senior IC or head of product working on something that matters, where AI fluency is fundamental to the culture.
